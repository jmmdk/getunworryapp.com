2. What Unworry is, and isn't

Unworry is a self-help tool for everyday worry. It offers exercises, a worry journal, a daily worry time, check-ins on whether feared outcomes happened, an SOS screen for difficult moments, and short lessons based on cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) and published research.

Unworry is not medical care, therapy or a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not replace a doctor, psychologist or other professional. The research summaries in the app are general information; studies vary in size and quality, and results for you may differ.

Seek professional help if worry or stress fills most of your day for several weeks, affects your sleep, appetite, work or relationships, or if daily life is not holding together. Helplines in the app are always free to access.
