Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version
  • What you write in Unworry (worries, journal, notes, exercise answers) is stored on your device. It is not uploaded to us or synced anywhere.
  • To create an account we store your e-mail address or your Apple or Google sign-in ID, on servers in the EU.
  • We collect basic usage statistics on EU servers, with IP addresses anonymised. They never include what you write.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • No AI processing of your content, no ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete account.

1. Who we are

Unworry is published by Useful Apps ApS, a company registered in Denmark ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: hi@getunworryapp.com

This policy covers the Unworry app for iOS and Android and the website getunworryapp.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The core of Unworry works on your phone. The following is stored only in the app's storage on your device:

  • the worries you capture, your predictions, topics, body signals and how strong the worry felt;
  • your "did it happen?" answers, your plans and notes, your journal and weekly reflections;
  • your exercise answers and before/after ratings;
  • your settings, such as name, theme, language, worry time and reminders.

We do not receive this content, and there is no cloud sync in this version of the app. Because we don't have it, we can't restore it if you lose your phone or delete the app. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings. That backup is governed by Apple's or Google's terms.

Dictation. If you tap the microphone to say a worry out loud, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition. Unworry does not record, receive or store the audio. Only the resulting text is kept, on your device. Depending on your device and its settings, the speech service provided by Apple or Google may process the audio under their own privacy terms. You can always type instead.

Reminders. Reminders (for example for your worry time or a check date) are local notifications scheduled on your device. We don't use server push notifications and don't collect push tokens.

3. Data we process

Your account

You create an account with a 6-digit code sent to your e-mail, with Sign in with Apple (iPhone) or with Google (Android). We store:

  • your e-mail address (if you use Sign in with Apple, this can be an Apple private relay address);
  • a user ID, the sign-in method, and when the account was created and last used;
  • technical login records, such as IP address and time of sign-in, kept for security.

We don't ask for your real name for the account. The name you can enter in onboarding stays on your device.

Purpose: to give you an account, keep it secure and link it to your subscription. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (art. 6(1)(f)).

Login e-mails

When you sign in with e-mail, we send the 6-digit code through an e-mail delivery provider. It processes your e-mail address and delivery data (for example whether the e-mail was delivered). Legal basis: contract (art. 6(1)(b)).

Usage statistics

To understand how the app is used and to improve it, the app sends a limited set of events: which screens are opened, steps in onboarding, that a worry was captured or checked, that a tool or the SOS screen was used, paywall views and purchases. Events can carry simple properties such as a screen path, a topic category, a 1–5 rating before and after an exercise, or which paywall version was shown. They also include basic device and app information (for example operating system, app version and language) and a random ID that is linked to your account ID after you sign in.

The events never include the text you write: no worries, notes, names or e-mail addresses. IP addresses are anonymised. We don't use session recording.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running, measuring and improving the app (art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by writing to us; see section 9.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or free trial is active. RevenueCat processes your account ID, purchase and subscription history (product, dates, price, currency, country, store transaction IDs) and basic device information. Legal basis: contract (art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal obligations for bookkeeping (art. 6(1)(c)).

Support

If you e-mail us, we process what you send us, such as your e-mail address and the content of your message, to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)) or contract, depending on the question. Please don't send us your worries or health details unless needed.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Account and analytics data are stored in the EU. Some providers, such as RevenueCat and GitHub, are based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional safeguards where needed.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't upload or read your worries, journal or notes.
  • We don't use AI to process your content.
  • We don't show ads and don't use advertising IDs or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data to anyone.
  • We don't use your data to make automated decisions that affect you legally or similarly.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Security logs are kept for a limited period.
  • Usage statistics: for as long as they are useful for improving the app, and no longer than 24 months.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide your subscription, and for the period required by Danish bookkeeping law (currently 5 years from the end of the financial year).
  • Support e-mails: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than 24 months after it is closed.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it, delete your account in the app, or delete the app.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes the data on your device (journal, exercises and settings) and your account with us. You can also e-mail hi@getunworryapp.com from the address linked to your account and ask us to delete it.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Purchase records we are legally required to keep are kept for that period only.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict our processing;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including usage statistics;
  • data portability for data you have given us.

To use your rights, write to hi@getunworryapp.com. We'll answer within one month. Remember that the content you write in the app is only on your device, so we can't give you a copy of it; you can see and delete it in the app.

You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

10. Age limit

Unworry is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our systems is limited and protected. Because your worries and journal stay on your device, protecting your phone with a passcode or biometric lock also protects them.

12. This website

getunworryapp.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking on the website, and fonts and images are served from our own domain. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to deliver the site; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you switch between light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser only.

13. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. We'll change the date at the top, and if the changes are significant we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
